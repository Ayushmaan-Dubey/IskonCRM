Temple CRM — Client Briefing

Security Enhancements Overview

System: temple-crm-web.onrender.com Status: All items below are live and active today

We've added three protections to the temple's system this update. None of them change how staff use the app day to day — no new steps, no extra clicks for normal work. They run quietly in the background and exist to protect the personal information of your donors, members, and newcomers, and to give you clear answers if you ever need to know "who did what, and when."

1. Activity Log

Active
A permanent record of every important action taken in the system

What it does

The system now automatically keeps a written record every time something important happens: someone logging in or out, a failed login attempt, a record being deleted, or a staff member's access level being changed. Each entry notes exactly who did it and exactly when.

Why it matters to you

If a donation record or a newcomer entry is ever accidentally deleted, or if a question ever comes up about who changed something, you have a clear, factual answer instead of relying on memory or guesswork. This is the same kind of accountability that banks and other organizations handling sensitive information are expected to maintain, and it protects your staff too — an honest volunteer can never be wrongly blamed for something the record shows they didn't do.

Who can see it

This log is only visible to your most senior administrator (Super Admin) — it's oversight information, not something regular staff need to see or manage.

2. Login Protection

Active
Automatically blocks repeated password-guessing attempts

What it does

If someone types the wrong password for an account five times in a row, that account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes. A staff member who simply mistypes their password once or twice is unaffected — they'll just see a friendly reminder of how many tries they have left.

Why it matters to you

This is one of the most common ways an online account gets broken into: a bad actor tries password after password until one works. That path is now closed. It requires no action from your staff — it simply activates on its own the moment repeated wrong attempts happen, and every lockout is written into the Activity Log above.

Think of it like: a door that automatically bolts itself for a short time after several wrong key attempts, instead of letting someone keep trying keys all night.

3. Secure Connection & Browser Safety Rules

Active
Every visit to the site is now encrypted, with extra safety rules enforced automatically

What it does

Two things happen automatically for every visitor now. First, the connection between a visitor's device and the temple's system is always encrypted (the padlock/HTTPS you see in a browser's address bar), and the system now insists on this — it will not allow an unprotected connection. Second, the system sends a set of instructions that every web browser understands and automatically follows, telling it things like "never let another website disguise itself as this one" and "never let this page be secretly embedded inside another site."

Why it matters to you

This closes off several well-known tricks used to intercept information in transit or fool a visitor into interacting with a fake or hidden version of the page. It's the same baseline of protection used by professional websites that handle payments and personal information, and — like the other two — it's already active and requires nothing further from you or your staff.

Summary

All three protections are live on the system today, verified working, and require no ongoing effort from the temple's staff. Together, they give you three things: a clear record of who did what, protection against someone trying to break into a staff account, and a secure, tamper-resistant connection for every visitor. This is a meaningful step toward handling your donors' and members' information with the same level of care they'd expect from any organization they trust with their personal details.
