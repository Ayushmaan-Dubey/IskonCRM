Quick Start Guide

Thank you for helping test the system today. It takes about two minutes to get started — no app to install, just a web browser.

temple-crm-web.onrender.com
Type this into your phone or laptop's browser. Works on WiFi or cellular data.

Step 1 — Create Your Account (once)

  1. Go to the link above, tap Sign Up.
  2. Choose your account type: Admin or Super Admin (see below for which one you are).
  3. Fill in your name, a username, email, phone number, and a password.
  4. Enter the PIN for your account type (table below).
Account TypeSignup PINNeeded at Login?
AdminRRNo — just email & password
Super Admin2026Yes — every time

Step 2 — Log In

Same page every time — enter your email and password. If you're a Super Admin, also enter PIN 2026 in the "Super Admin PIN" box (Admins can leave that box blank).

Heads up: 5 wrong attempts locks the account for 15 minutes

This is a real security feature we just added, not a bug. If you mistype your password, take it slow — you'll see a warning showing attempts remaining before it locks.

What to Try — Everyone

Add Newcomer

Log a visitor's info: name, phone, WhatsApp interest, how they heard about us.

Add Sponsor

Log a donation/sponsorship. Start typing a name — if that person is already in the system, a suggestion appears so you can autofill instead of retyping.

My Data / Donation Form

"My Data" shows everything you've personally entered. "Donation Form" is the public page devotees would use to submit a donation themselves — check it looks right.

What to Try — Admins Only

Look for the Admin menu in the top navigation:

  • Reports — full newcomer & sponsorship tables
  • People — the master list of everyone, tagged Visitor/Donator/Member
  • Home Program Outreach and Inventory

What to Try — Super Admin Only

Same Admin menu, with extra items only you can see:

  • Import Spreadsheet — bulk-upload a .xlsx of people
  • Fact Sheet and Payment Settings
  • Security Log — a record of every login, logout, and change made today

If Something Looks Wrong

Note what you were doing and what happened (a screenshot helps a lot), and let the organizer know. Don't worry about breaking anything — this is exactly what today is for.
